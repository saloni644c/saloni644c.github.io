Research

Knee MRI abnormality detection from radiology reports

Weakly supervised, multi-label detection of 12 knee findings on 3D knee MRI. An instruction-tuned LLM (MedGemma-4B) extracts probabilistic labels from free-text reports in 9 languages, with verbatim evidence quotes so every label is auditable. Labels are validated against 58 doctor-verified cases per finding and per language (AUC, F1, Cohen's kappa). ACL validated at AUC 0.944; remaining findings preliminary. Imaging model follows an MRNet-style multi-label CNN trained on the extracted labels.
